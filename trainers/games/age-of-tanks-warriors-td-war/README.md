# Age of Tanks Warriors: TD War

Trainer profile for **Age of Tanks Warriors: TD War** on CrazyGames.

---

## Features

* **Turbo Battle** — Accelerate internal time dilation by 5x to rapidly regenerate Food/Energy and boost cannon turret fire rates.
* **Timescale Speedhack** — Cycle battle tempo between 1.0x, 2.0x, and 5.0x.
* **Instant Ad-Reward Bypass** — Background integration to claim in-game reward boxes, card packs, and coin multipliers without watching ads.

---

## Installation

Install the global loader script in Tampermonkey:
👉 **[Install `loader.user.js`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kentiers/web-trainers/main/trainers/loader.user.js)**

The trainer HUD will appear automatically when opening the game. Press **`INSERT`** to show or hide the menu.
